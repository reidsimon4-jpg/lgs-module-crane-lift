Infinity Living Options (ILO) - lift engineering study, Rev D (final design)

PRELIMINARY - subject to structural engineering. Not for construction.

One classroom module: 12.0 m long, 3.3 m wide, about 3.3 m high, framed in 0.95 mm C89 steel sections joined with screws. It is picked up by a crane at 8 lift points. This page shows the final Rev D design. Each piece of steel below is coloured by how hard it works during the lift. We call this its utilisation: the force in the part divided by what it can safely carry. 1.00 means the part is exactly at its design capacity, with all safety factors included.       This page does not approve the module for lifting. A registered structural engineer must review, adopt and sign the design; the rig must be certified and proof tested; the hold points (weighing with load cells, hang angle stop, landing on continuous bearers and the others listed in "How it works") must be met. The calculations were produced with AI assistance under ILO's direction and have not been checked or certified by a registered engineer.
